\cvsection{Research Interests}
Usable privacy and security; human-AI interaction; privacy in LLM
applications; AI adoption and trust.

\cvsection{Research Profile}
My research studies how people understand, negotiate, and protect their
privacy as AI systems and connected devices enter everyday settings. I
combine large-scale surveys and experiments with statistical modeling to
identify what users need to make informed privacy decisions, and translate
those findings into design mechanisms for privacy negotiation and
disclosure. My recent work focuses on privacy leakage in LLM applications
and on how users weigh privacy, trust, and utility when adopting AI
assistants.
