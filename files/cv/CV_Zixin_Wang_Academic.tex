\cvsection{Research Interests}
Human-centered security; human-AI interaction; usable privacy and security.
\par\vspace{0.35em}
I study how people negotiate privacy and control with other people and with
AI systems: what guests expect from a host's smart home devices, what users
are willing to tell a chatbot, and how people keep oversight as AI agents
begin to act on their behalf. I work mainly with large-scale surveys and
vignette experiments, and turn the findings into negotiation mechanisms,
notices, and controls that fit how people actually use these systems.
